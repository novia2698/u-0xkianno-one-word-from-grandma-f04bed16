How the entry works: a Node 22 stdlib-only program, main.ts, writes the single word to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt (default /output/entry.txt), validates it first against the gate (1–30 characters, not only white space, exactly one word), and writes it byte for byte with no trailing newline. The word itself: "ChatGTP" — ChatGPT's name with the T and the P swapped, the classic typo of a grandma typing a word she has only ever heard spoken. The judge compares words two at a time and picks the funnier, more grandma-like one, weighing in order: reads as the word a grandma would text about AI; warm and a little baffled grandmother's voice; funny in one word with no caption; tied to AI, robots or machines; fresh rather than obvious, still instantly readable. "ChatGTP" hits all five: it is the AI's own name, the typo is the grandma voice, the joke lands on reading, it names the AI itself, and the misspelling is what makes it fresh instead of the obvious correctly spelled pick.
